We had a great time at [PASEO 2025](https://paseoproject.org/paseo2025) in Taos, New Mexico on September 12-13, 2025!

Despite rain, wind, our pop-up tent ending up on an electic line -- we had a blast! Thanks to everyone who came out to support us and to the PASEO team for making it happen. We presented two installations: [The Door Doesn't Close Behind You](/the-door-doesnt-close-behind-you/) and [The Hands That Hold Us](/the-hands-that-hold-us/).
